What the Service Does

Protection designed for demanding wheel surfaces.

Alloy wheels face brake dust, road film, salt and repeated heat cycles. A suitable wheel coating creates a durable, easy-clean surface, helping contamination release more readily during correct maintenance.

This is a protection service, not a wheel refurbishment. Existing kerb damage, corrosion, peeling lacquer or permanent staining will not be repaired by ceramic coating. We will assess the wheels and explain what is realistically achievable before confirming the work.

Typically included

  • Initial wheel condition assessment
  • Thorough wheel and tyre cleaning
  • Decontamination where appropriate
  • Careful drying and surface preparation
  • Application of a suitable ceramic wheel coating
  • Curing and aftercare guidance
Key Benefits

Why ceramic coat alloy wheels?

01

Easier cleaning

Brake dust and road grime can be easier to remove during regular, safe wheel washing.

02

Added protection

A durable sacrificial layer helps protect suitable wheel finishes from routine contamination.

03

Enhanced finish

Correct preparation and coating can improve gloss and help well-kept wheels stay looking their best.

Frequently Asked Questions

Alloy wheel coating questions

How much does alloy wheel ceramic coating cost?

Pricing depends on wheel size, design, condition and the preparation required. Send photographs through the enquiry form and we can provide an appropriate quotation.

Do the wheels need to be removed?

The correct approach depends on the wheel design and the areas being coated. We will explain the proposed process when assessing your vehicle.

Will coating repair damaged alloy wheels?

No. Ceramic coating does not repair kerb damage, corrosion, peeling lacquer or structural defects. Refurbishment should be completed before coating where necessary.

Can this be added to a ceramic coating package?

Yes, wheel protection can be discussed alongside paint ceramic coating, subject to inspection and product suitability.

How long will the coating last?

Durability varies with preparation, product choice, mileage, winter use and maintenance. We will provide realistic guidance for the selected coating.

Which areas do you cover?

We provide mobile detailing across Bourne, Peterborough, Stamford, Grantham, Market Deeping and surrounding areas.
