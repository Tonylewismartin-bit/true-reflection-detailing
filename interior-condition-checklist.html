Car Interior Condition Checklist

Use this before asking for a mobile interior clean. It helps you describe the job, take useful photos and understand what may affect the quote. You can print it or tick the boxes on your phone.

Start the checklist

1. Check each part of the car

Tick the areas that need attention. Check the boot and the less visible places as well as the seats.

Seats and surfaces

Carpets and difficult areas

Spills and smells

Vehicle and access

2. Take photos that show the work

Whole interior

Photograph the front seats, rear seats, footwells and boot so the vehicle size and overall condition are clear.

Close-ups

Show pet hair, stains or damage in natural light. Include one wider photo so we can locate each problem.

Tell us the history

Say what caused a stain if known, when it happened and whether you have already tried cleaning it.

3. Set realistic expectations

Cleaning can improve many interiors, but permanent staining, material damage and smells with an unresolved source may remain. Heavy pet hair and extensive staining can take additional time. A photo assessment helps us suggest the right service and confirm a quote.

If you also want the outside of the car cleaned, compare the Mobile Full Valet. For a focused interior service, see what is included in our Interior Deep Clean.

Ready to ask for a quote?

Send your vehicle details, location, photos and the areas you ticked above through our enquiry form. Interior Deep Clean starts from £150; the final price depends on the vehicle and work required.
