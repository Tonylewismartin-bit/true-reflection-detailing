What paint correction can and cannot do

Light marks and swirls

Machine polishing can often improve defects in the clear coat and restore clarity. The safe result depends on paint thickness, condition and the depth of the marks.

Deep scratches and chips

Polishing cannot replace missing paint. Some deep marks may be softened visually, while others require a body repair or repainting. An inspection is the right first step.

Autoglym describes clear-coat rectification for swirls and light scratches, and advises professional assessment for deeper scratches. Read its product guidance.

How to photograph the mark for an assessment

  1. Take one photo showing the whole panel and where the mark sits.
  2. Take a close-up in even daylight, avoiding filters and heavy reflections.
  3. Take a second close-up from a different angle and tell us whether the panel has been repainted or polished before, if known.
