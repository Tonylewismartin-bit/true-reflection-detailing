Interior Restoration

A Deeper Clean for Everyday Use, Family Cars and Heavily Used Interiors.

Dust, crumbs, spills, pet hair and everyday use gradually build up in carpets, seats, seams and hard-to-reach areas. Our interior deep clean is designed to refresh the cabin methodically rather than simply mask dirt or odours.

Every vehicle is assessed first. The time, methods and final price depend on its size, materials and condition, including the level of staining, pet hair and contamination.

What Is Included

Detailed Interior Cleaning, Surface by Surface.

The exact process is adapted to your vehicle and the condition of its interior.

  • Detailed interior vacuum
  • Carpet and mat deep cleaning
  • Fabric seat cleaning
  • Extraction where appropriate
  • Leather cleansing
  • Dashboard and trim cleaning
  • Door cards, pockets and sills
  • Interior glass cleaning
  • Crevices and hard-to-reach areas
  • Suitable interior surface protection
Common Interior Problems

What We Can Help Improve.

Stains & Spills

Suitable stains can often be improved through careful cleaning and extraction, although permanent staining or material damage cannot always be removed completely.

Pet Hair

Embedded pet hair takes additional time and may affect the quotation. Clear photographs help us assess the likely work before confirming a booking.

Unpleasant Odours

Cleaning can remove many sources of odour, but persistent smells may require specialist treatment and cannot be guaranteed without identifying the cause.

Our Process

Every Interior Is Assessed First.

1. Condition ReviewWe review the interior materials, stains, pet hair, odours and areas needing particular attention.
2. Thorough VacuumingLoose dirt and debris are removed from carpets, seats, storage areas and accessible crevices.
3. Surface-Specific CleaningFabrics, carpets, leather and trim are cleaned using suitable products and methods.
4. Inspection & FinishingThe interior is checked, glass is cleaned and appropriate surfaces are finished and protected.
Realistic Results

Can Every Stain and Odour Be Removed?

Not always. Results depend on the material, age of the contamination, previous cleaning attempts and whether a substance has permanently dyed or damaged the surface.

We will explain what is realistically achievable and avoid aggressive treatment that could damage the interior.

Questions Answered

Interior Deep Clean FAQs.

How much does a car interior deep clean cost?

Prices start from £150. A firm quotation depends on vehicle size, condition, staining, pet hair and the level of work required.

How long does an interior deep clean take?

Time varies according to the vehicle and its condition. We confirm the expected duration after assessing your photographs and requirements.

Can you remove pet hair?

Yes, we can remove pet hair, although heavily embedded hair is time-consuming and may increase the price.

Do you clean leather seats?

Yes. Leather is cleaned using appropriate products and methods, with suitable protection applied where included.

Will seats and carpets be wet afterwards?

Where extraction is appropriate, materials may remain slightly damp. Drying time depends on the material, weather and ventilation.

Is this a mobile service?

Yes. We provide mobile interior detailing across Bourne, Peterborough, Stamford, Grantham, Market Deeping and surrounding areas.
