Questions Answered

Car Detailing FAQs.

Clear answers to common questions about ceramic coating, machine polishing, paint correction and premium mobile car valeting.

How much does ceramic coating cost in the UK?

Pricing varies according to vehicle size, paint condition, preparation, correction work and the coating system selected. We provide a vehicle-specific recommendation.

Can machine polishing remove deep scratches?

Machine polishing can reduce some clear-coat defects, but deeper scratches may only be improved rather than removed. If a defect is through the clear coat or paint, polishing alone may not repair it.

How do you remove swirl marks from car paint?

Swirl marks are usually improved through careful machine polishing after the paint has been washed, decontaminated and inspected. The safest approach depends on paint thickness and condition.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Durability depends on the coating used, preparation, mileage, storage conditions and how the vehicle is washed and maintained.
