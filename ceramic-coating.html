Why Ceramic Coating?

Professional Ceramic Coating for Long-Term Paint Protection

A ceramic coating forms a durable protective layer over properly prepared paintwork. It can improve gloss, water behaviour and resistance to everyday contamination, while making routine washing easier.

The coating is only as good as the preparation underneath it. That's why we assess the vehicle first and recommend the level of preparation needed rather than treating every car the same.

01

Enhanced Gloss

Proper preparation and ceramic protection can leave paintwork with a deeper, cleaner and more refined appearance.

02

Easier Maintenance

Hydrophobic behaviour can help water and everyday contamination move away from the surface more readily.

03

Long-Term Protection

A professionally applied coating provides a longer-lasting protective finish than a conventional wax alone.

Our Process

Our Ceramic Coating Process Starts With Preparation

We don't simply wash a vehicle and apply a coating. The condition of the paint determines the preparation required.

1. Vehicle AssessmentWe inspect the paintwork and discuss how the vehicle is used, maintained and what you want to achieve.
2. Safe Wash & DecontaminationThe vehicle is thoroughly cleaned and chemically decontaminated to prepare the surface.
3. Machine Polishing Where RequiredExisting defects and paint condition are assessed. Machine polishing can be recommended where it will improve the final result.
4. Ceramic Coating ApplicationThe prepared paintwork is carefully coated and allowed to cure according to the product requirements.
What Ceramic Coating Can & Can't Do

What Ceramic Coating Can — and Can't — Do

It Can Help With

Gloss, water behaviour, easier maintenance and protection from everyday environmental contamination.

It Won't Prevent

Stone chips, deep scratches or all physical damage. A coating should not be sold as an invisible armour layer.

Preparation Matters

Applying a coating over neglected or heavily marked paint can lock in imperfections. We assess the surface first.

Ceramic Coating vs Wax

Ceramic Coating vs Wax

Wax can be an excellent short-term protection option and is often ideal for regular maintenance. Ceramic coating is aimed at owners looking for longer-lasting protection and easier maintenance.

The right choice depends on the vehicle, its condition, how it is used and how frequently you want to maintain it. We'll explain the options before any work begins.

Ceramic Coating Aftercare

How to Look After Your Ceramic Coating

Ceramic coating still needs proper maintenance. Safe washing methods, suitable products and regular maintenance help preserve the finish and water behaviour over time.

  • Use safe, paint-friendly washing methods.
  • Avoid harsh chemicals unless they are appropriate for the coating.
  • Keep the vehicle regularly maintained rather than allowing contamination to build up.
  • Follow the aftercare advice provided when the coating is installed.
Frequently Asked Questions

Ceramic Coating FAQs

How much does ceramic coating cost in the UK?

The cost depends on vehicle size, paint condition, preparation and the coating selected. We assess the vehicle before providing a firm quotation.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Durability depends on the coating product, preparation, vehicle use and maintenance. We explain the expected protection period for the coating recommended.

Can machine polishing be included?

Yes. If the paint would benefit from correction or enhancement before coating, machine polishing can be included in the preparation.

How do I wash a ceramic coated car?

Use a safe contact wash routine and coating-compatible products. Avoid aggressive washing methods that can damage the paint or coating.

Can you ceramic coat wheels and glass?

Additional surfaces can be considered depending on the vehicle and protection package selected.
