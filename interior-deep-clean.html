Seats, Carpets & Leather

Car Seat, Carpet & Leather Cleaning.

A car interior deep clean takes account of the surface and its condition, rather than treating every part of the cabin in the same way.

Car Seat & Upholstery Cleaning

Fabric seats can collect crumbs, everyday grime and drink spills around seams and seating areas. Upholstery cleaning targets this build-up, with extraction where the material and condition make it appropriate. Old stains may remain visible even after cleaning.

Carpet & Mat Cleaning

Vacuuming removes loose debris before deeper carpet cleaning tackles ground-in dirt in footwells and mats. We pay attention to accessible edges and crevices. Wear, discolouration and damaged fibres cannot be repaired by cleaning.

Leather Seat Cleaning

Leather needs a different approach from fabric upholstery. Suitable products and methods help lift surface dirt from seating areas and seams. Cleaning does not repair cracks, scuffs or worn colour; the condition of the finish guides what is appropriate.

Common Interior Problems

Stains, Pet Hair & Odours: Realistic Results.

Stains & Spills

Stain and spill removal starts with the material and what was spilled. Food and drink marks can often be improved with suitable cleaning and, where appropriate, extraction. Results depend on how long the spill has been present and whether it has permanently discoloured or damaged the surface. Complete removal is not guaranteed.

Pet Hair

Pet hair removal focuses on affected upholstery, carpets and accessible crevices. Hair woven into fibres can be difficult to release, and complete removal is not always possible. Heavy build-up takes extra time and may affect the quotation, so include photographs before booking.

Unpleasant Odours

Cleaning aims to remove accessible sources of unpleasant odours, such as food residue and dirt, rather than cover them with fragrance. Persistent smoke, damp or deeply absorbed spills may need further investigation or specialist treatment. A deep clean cannot guarantee odour removal or resolve an underlying leak.

Questions Answered

Interior Deep Clean FAQs.

How much does a car interior deep clean cost?

Prices start from £150. Vehicle size, condition, staining, pet hair and the work required determine the final quotation. Send photographs for a tailored quote.

How long does an interior deep clean take?

We confirm the expected cleaning time after reviewing your photographs and requirements. Allow additional time for seats and carpets to dry when planning your booking.

Does car seat and carpet cleaning include shampooing or extraction?

Fabric seat, upholstery, carpet and mat cleaning are included, along with detailed vacuuming. The method depends on the material and condition; extraction is used where appropriate, rather than on every surface.

Can you remove stains from car seats?

Food, drink and everyday marks can often be improved, but complete removal is not guaranteed. Old stains, permanent dye transfer and material damage may remain. Tell us what caused the mark and about previous cleaning attempts.

Can you remove dog hair from car seats and carpets?

We offer pet hair removal from upholstery, carpets and accessible areas. Heavily embedded hair takes additional time, may increase the price and cannot always be removed completely. Include photographs of the affected areas.

Do you clean leather seats?

Yes. We use products and methods suited to the leather, with suitable protection where included. Cleaning lifts surface dirt but does not repair cracks, scuffs or worn colour.

Can a deep clean remove smoke or damp smells?

Cleaning can help when the source is accessible dirt or residue. Smoke, damp or spills beneath the surface may need specialist treatment or investigation. Odour removal is not guaranteed, and cleaning cannot fix an underlying leak.

Can you clean heavily soiled car interiors?

These are assessed individually before booking. Send photographs and describe old spills, ground-in dirt, pet hair and persistent smells. We explain the likely work, price and limitations, including when specialist attention is needed.

Will car seats and carpets be wet afterwards?

Materials may remain damp after extraction. Drying depends on the fabric, cleaning required, weather and ventilation. We discuss drying with you; allow time before normal use rather than relying on a fixed drying-time promise.

Do you offer mobile interior cleaning in Bourne and Peterborough?

Yes. We cover Bourne, Peterborough, Stamford, Grantham, Market Deeping and surrounding areas. Send your location so we can confirm booking arrangements and any access or preparation requirements.
