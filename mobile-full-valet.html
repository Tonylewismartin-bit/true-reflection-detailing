Why Does the Final Price Vary?

The starting price applies to a standard-sized vehicle in a condition consistent with the service. Vehicle size, pet hair, staining, odours, embedded dirt, paint contamination and additional treatments all affect the time required.

Clear photographs allow us to provide a fair quotation before confirming the appointment.

Questions Answered

Mobile Full Valet FAQs.

How much does a full car valet cost?

Our Mobile Full Valet starts from £225. We confirm the price after reviewing the vehicle size, condition and photographs.

How long does a full valet take?

Allow approximately 4–6 hours or longer. Heavily soiled interiors and larger vehicles may require additional time.

Is paint correction included?

No. Paint correction and machine polishing are specialist services. We can assess the paint and quote separately if required.

Does it include stain removal?

Suitable stains will be treated, but permanent staining or material damage cannot always be removed safely.

Can you remove pet hair?

Yes, although heavily embedded pet hair requires additional time and may increase the quotation.

Do you bring water and power?

Yes. True Reflection Detailing is set up as a self-sufficient mobile service, subject to suitable and safe working conditions.
