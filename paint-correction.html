What Is Paint Correction?

Professional Machine Polishing & Paint Correction

Paint correction uses machine polishing to refine the clear coat and reduce suitable defects. Depending on the condition of the vehicle, this can improve gloss and reduce the appearance of swirl marks, light scratches, haze and other paint imperfections.

Correction is not about removing as much paint as possible. A professional approach balances the desired improvement with the amount of clear coat that can safely be removed.

01

Swirl Mark Reduction

Machine polishing can significantly reduce suitable wash-induced swirls and improve the clarity of the paintwork.

02

Gloss Enhancement

Removing suitable surface defects and refining the paint can produce a deeper, cleaner and more reflective finish.

03

Paint-Safe Approach

We assess the paint before polishing and avoid promising correction where a defect is too deep or unsafe to remove.

Enhancement vs Correction

Paint Enhancement vs Paint Correction

Paint Enhancement

A lighter polishing process designed primarily to improve gloss and refine the finish while reducing suitable minor defects.

Single-Stage Correction

A more focused correction process aimed at achieving a greater level of defect reduction while retaining a sensible paint-safety margin.

More Extensive Correction

Some vehicles require additional stages. The achievable result depends on paint hardness, defect depth, previous repairs and remaining clear coat.

A Realistic Approach

Can Machine Polishing Remove Deep Scratches?

Not always. If a scratch has penetrated deeply into the clear coat or paint, attempting to polish it completely away may remove too much material. Some deeper defects may only be safely reduced rather than completely removed.

We would rather give you an honest assessment of what is achievable than promise a perfect finish that could compromise the paint.

Swirl Marks

How to Remove Swirl Marks From Car Paint

Swirl marks are commonly caused by poor washing and drying techniques. Machine polishing can reduce suitable swirls by refining the upper layer of the clear coat, but the amount of correction possible varies from vehicle to vehicle.

The best long-term solution is not simply correcting the paint — it is also changing the washing routine that caused the defects in the first place.

Frequently Asked Questions

Paint Correction FAQs

How much does paint correction cost?

Pricing depends on vehicle size, paint condition, defect severity and the level of correction required. We assess the vehicle before providing a firm quotation.

Can machine polishing remove deep scratches?

Some scratches can be reduced, but a deep scratch may be below the level that can safely be corrected by polishing. We will explain what is realistically achievable.

Can you remove swirl marks?

Suitable wash-induced swirl marks can often be significantly reduced with machine polishing. The exact result depends on the paint and defect severity.

Is paint correction safe?

Professional correction is based on controlled paint removal and should be approached conservatively. We assess the vehicle and avoid unnecessary correction.

Should I have ceramic coating after paint correction?

Correction creates an ideal opportunity to apply protection. Ceramic coating is an option for owners wanting longer-lasting protection and easier maintenance.

Do you provide machine polishing as a mobile service?

Yes. We provide mobile paint correction and machine polishing across Bourne, Peterborough, Stamford, Grantham, Market Deeping and surrounding areas, subject to suitable working conditions.
