Seats, Carpets & Leather

Care That Suits the Material.

A car interior deep clean takes account of the surface and its condition, rather than treating every part of the cabin in the same way.

Car Seat & Upholstery Cleaning

Fabric seats can collect crumbs, everyday grime and drink spills around seams and seating areas. Upholstery cleaning targets this build-up, with extraction where the material and condition make it appropriate. Old stains may remain visible even after cleaning.

Carpet & Mat Cleaning

Vacuuming removes loose debris before deeper carpet cleaning tackles ground-in dirt in footwells and mats. We pay attention to accessible edges and crevices. Wear, discolouration and damaged fibres cannot be repaired by cleaning.

Leather Seat Cleaning

Leather needs a different approach from fabric upholstery. Suitable products and methods help lift surface dirt from seating areas and seams. Cleaning does not repair cracks, scuffs or worn colour; the condition of the finish guides what is appropriate.

Common Interior Problems

What We Can Help Improve.

Stains & Spills

Stain and spill removal starts with the material and what was spilled. Food and drink marks can often be improved with suitable cleaning and, where appropriate, extraction. Results depend on how long the spill has been present and whether it has permanently discoloured or damaged the surface. Complete removal is not guaranteed.

Pet Hair

Pet hair removal focuses on affected upholstery, carpets and accessible crevices. Hair woven into fibres can be difficult to release, and complete removal is not always possible. Heavy build-up takes extra time and may affect the quotation, so include photographs before booking.

Unpleasant Odours

Cleaning aims to remove accessible sources of unpleasant odours, such as food residue and dirt, rather than cover them with fragrance. Persistent smoke, damp or deeply absorbed spills may need further investigation or specialist treatment. A deep clean cannot guarantee odour removal or resolve an underlying leak.

Questions Answered

Interior Deep Clean FAQs.

How much does a car interior deep clean cost?

Prices start from £150. A firm quotation depends on vehicle size, condition, staining, pet hair and the level of work required. Send photos for a tailored quote.

How long does an interior deep clean take?

Time varies according to the vehicle and its condition. We confirm the expected duration after assessing your photographs and requirements. Allow for drying time as well as the cleaning itself when planning your booking.

Does the service include car seat and carpet cleaning?

Yes. Fabric seat and upholstery cleaning, carpet and mat cleaning, and detailed vacuuming are included. Extraction is used where appropriate to the material and condition; leather seats need a different cleaning method.

Can you remove stains from car seats?

Suitable cleaning can often improve food, drink and everyday marks, but complete stain removal is not guaranteed. The material, age of the spill and previous treatment all affect the result. Permanent dye transfer, wear and material damage may remain. Tell us what caused the mark if you know.

Can you remove pet hair?

We offer pet hair removal from upholstery, carpets and accessible areas. Heavily embedded hair takes additional time, may increase the price and cannot always be removed completely. Send clear photographs of the affected seats and carpets so we can assess the work.

Do you clean leather seats?

Yes. Leather is cleaned using appropriate products and methods, with suitable protection applied where included. Cleaning can improve surface dirt but does not repair cracks, scuffs or worn colour.

Can a deep clean remove unpleasant odours?

Cleaning can help where the source is accessible dirt or residue. Smoke, damp and spills absorbed beneath the surface can be harder to resolve and may require specialist treatment or investigation. We assess the likely cause and explain the limitations; odour removal is not guaranteed.

Can you clean heavily soiled interiors?

Heavily soiled interiors are assessed individually before a booking is confirmed. Send photographs and describe ground-in dirt, old spills, pet hair and any persistent smells. The condition affects the methods, time and price, and we will explain if the work needs specialist attention.

Will seats and carpets be wet afterwards?

Where extraction is appropriate, materials may remain damp. Drying time depends on the fabric, the cleaning required, weather and ventilation. Allow time for drying before normal use; we will discuss this with you rather than promise an exact drying time.

Is this a mobile service in Bourne and Peterborough?

Yes. We provide mobile interior detailing across Bourne, Peterborough, Stamford, Grantham, Market Deeping and surrounding areas. Send your location with your enquiry so we can confirm the booking arrangements.

What should I send for a quote?

Send your vehicle details, location and clear photographs of the seats, footwells, mats and any stains or pet hair. Describe odours as well, since these cannot be assessed from photographs alone. Mention the source of any spill and previous cleaning attempts where known.

How should I prepare the car?

Remove personal belongings and loose items so the interior can be assessed and cleaned. Point out delicate or damaged areas and any problems not obvious in photographs. We will confirm any other access or preparation requirements with your booking.
