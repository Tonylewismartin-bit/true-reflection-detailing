Peterborough Mobile Detailing Questions

Do you bring water and electricity?

We bring our own power and filtered water. We still need safe access, enough working space and permission to use the parking location.

How much does mobile valeting cost?

Maintenance is £60. An interior deep clean starts from £150 and a full valet from £225. Paint enhancement with ceramic coating starts from £450. Assessment-based quotes depend on size, condition and the agreed work.

Can you remove every stain or scratch?

No. Some stains and odours may remain. Machine polishing can improve suitable shallow paint defects, but cannot replace missing paint or repair failing clear coat.
